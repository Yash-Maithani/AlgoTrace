Input Format Guide

For BFS/DFS (Unweighted):
First line: [number of nodes] [number of edges]
Next lines: [source] [target] for each edge
Last line: [start node]

Example:
5 5
A B
A C
B D
C E
D E
A

For Dijkstra/Prim:
First line: [number of nodes] [number of edges]
Next lines: [source] [target] [weight] for each edge
Last line: [start node]

Example:
5 7
A B 4
A C 2
B C 5
B D 10
C D 3
C E 7
D E 6
A

For Kruskal:
First line: [number of nodes] [number of edges]
Next lines: [source] [target] [weight] for each edge
(No start node needed)

Example:
5 7
A B 4
A C 2
B C 5
B D 10
C D 3
C E 7
D E 6

For Floyd-Warshall:
First line: [number of nodes] [number of edges]
Next lines: [source] [target] [weight] for each edge
Negative weights allowed

Example:
4 6
A B 3
A C 8
A D -4
B D 7
C B 4
D C 6

For Bellman-Ford:
First line: [number of nodes] [number of edges]
Next lines: [source] [target] [weight] for each edge
Last line: [start node] — negative weights allowed

Example:
5 6
A B 6
A C 7
B C 8
B D 5
C E 9
D E -3
A
